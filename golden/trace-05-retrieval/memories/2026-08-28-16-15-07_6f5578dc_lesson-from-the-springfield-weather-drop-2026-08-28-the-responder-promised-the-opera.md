Lesson from the Springfield weather drop (2026-08-28): the responder promised the operator 'I'm on it' twice, but the fetch never actually ran — the responder is a fast reply-only thinker with no tools, so a promise of real work (research/fetch/computation) only lands if the monolith picks the message up and does the act.
