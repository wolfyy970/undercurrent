## Wire-level differences (informational: headers + raw body)

Request headers that differ (scenarios): `content-length` ×254, `user-agent` ×254, `accept-encoding` ×254, `accept-language` ×254, `connection` ×254, `sec-fetch-mode` ×254, `expect` ×1. curl sends `User-Agent: curl/…`, `Accept: */*` and (bodies ≥ 1 MiB) `Expect: 100-continue`; node adds `accept-encoding`, `accept-language`, `connection`, `sec-fetch-mode`, `user-agent: node`; `content-length` differs because jq pretty-prints the payload and JSON.stringify does not.
